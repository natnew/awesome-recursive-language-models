# Code of Conduct

Help keep `awesome-recursive-language-models` professional, respectful, and useful.
This policy applies to contributors and maintainers across project spaces.

## Expected Behaviour

- Communicate courteously, disagree constructively, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect others' time, privacy, and perspectives; avoid personal attacks.
- Submit relevant, accurate, readable content and check files and links before submitting.
- Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and correct mistakes when they are identified.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Trolling, baiting, spam, or deliberately disrupting discussions and reviews.
- Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, or sharing private information without consent.
- Submitting malicious files, knowingly corrupted content, fabricated sources, or deceptive links.
- Repeatedly submitting rejected content without addressing feedback, or retaliating against reporters.

Honest mistakes are opportunities to improve; deliberate abuse or repeated disregard
for feedback may lead to moderation.

## Reporting a Concern

Report concerns privately to `opensourcereporting.ai@proton.me`.
Include a brief description and relevant links or evidence. Report security vulnerabilities
via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers will assess reports fairly, protect privacy, and respond proportionately.
Responses may include warnings, content removal, rejected contributions, or restrictions
on participation.
